# Code of conduct

Allstill is meant to be calm, and so is working on it. This applies wherever people meet around Allstill: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, also when you disagree. Argue about the work, never about the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Help people who are new. Everyone was once.
- Accept that a decision is the maintainer's, and that "not now" is an answer too.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, and remarks about someone's identity, background or body.
- Sexual language or attention that is not wanted.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Keeping on after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Write to [conduct@allstill.dev](mailto:conduct@allstill.dev). Only the maintainer reads it, and what you write stays between you and the maintainer unless you agree otherwise.

You hear back within 7 days. Depending on what happened, the maintainer may talk to the person, remove what they wrote, or block them from the project, for a while or for good.
